    Your donation is tax deductible

    Every contribution to What a Trip is tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by U.S. law.

    What a Trip is a fiscally sponsored project of Creative Visions Foundation, a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.

    Donations are processed by Creative Visions’ platform partner, Pledge.to, through the Pledge Foundation, a registered 501(c)(3). Your gift is received and receipted through the Pledge Foundation, so your emailed receipt and tax‑ID acknowledgment will come from them. The funds are then transferred to Creative Visions Foundation and, after platform and fiscal‑sponsorship fees, granted to What a Trip.

    You can give by cash, ACH bank transfer, stock, donor-advised fund (DAF), crypto, or credit card. ACH transfers carry the lowest processing fees, so more of your gift goes straight to the film.

    Right after you give, you’ll receive an emailed receipt to keep for your tax records. No goods or services are provided in exchange for your donation, except as otherwise noted for specific perks or rewards.

    Please consult your tax advisor about your particular situation.
