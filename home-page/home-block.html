FAQs

What is the film about?

What a Trip follows Rick Doblin and MAPS, the organization he founded, across a 40+ year effort to bring psychedelic therapy back into culture.

Who is making it?

The film is directed by Nirvan Mullick, produced by Dream Mullick, and executive produced by Darren Aronofsky.

How long has it been in the works?

Twelve years. We have been filming with Rick since 2014.

Where is the film now?

Filming is largely done and we are editing the rough cut, one act at a time. The budget section shows which milestone we are funding right now.

When will it be finished?

We are aiming for late 2027. The exact timing depends on how quickly the edit is funded.

How is the film funded?

By donations, together with years of unpaid work and investment from the filmmakers. The total budget is $3.4M: $1.8M already raised and invested since 2014, $700K for the rough cut, and $900K for the final cut.

What does my donation pay for?

Right now, editing. Every $50 covers about one hour of an editor’s time. The budget section lists what each milestone pays for.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Donations go through Creative Visions Foundation, our nonprofit fiscal sponsor, and are tax-deductible, less the fair market value of any perk you receive.

What is TRUTH Not DARE?

It is the film’s impact campaign, launching alongside the film. It offers honest education about the real risks and benefits of psychedelics. Read more.
